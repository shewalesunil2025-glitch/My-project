# Google Play listing — ibaxai

Copy each box into Play Console → **Grow → Store presence → Main store listing**. Limits: app name 30
characters, short description 80, full description 4000. No prices in the text or screenshots
(prices change and differ by country). Add Hindi under **Manage translations → Hindi (hi-IN)**.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| App name | `ibaxai: AI Business Assistant` (29) |
| Default language | English (India) – en-IN |
| App or game | App |
| Free or paid | Free (services are bought on the website — see checklist B4) |
| Category | Business |
| Tags | Business, Productivity, Communication |
| Contact email | the support email in `siteConfig.email` |
| Website | https://www.ibaxai.com |
| Privacy policy | https://www.ibaxai.com/privacy |

```
ibaxai is your AI business assistant. Tell IBAX what you need in your own words — in English, Hindi, Marathi or any language — and it switches on the right service for your business. Just say it. IBAX does it.

Made for small businesses: restaurants, clinics, salons, shops, hotels, gyms, real estate and more. No technical work, no code.

WHAT IBAX DOES FOR YOU

• WhatsApp Automation – IBAX replies to your customers on your own WhatsApp number in seconds, day and night, from your own prices, timings and FAQs. When a customer wants to book, pay or complain, IBAX hands the chat to you and alerts you. When you reply yourself, IBAX steps back.

• Customer Support – instant answers to common questions on your website chat and WhatsApp, and an alert to you when a customer needs a person.

• Lead Follow-up – every new enquiry gets a thank-you on WhatsApp and gentle reminders, and stops as soon as they reply.

• Google Review Management – happy customers get a one-tap Google review link on WhatsApp, and IBAX writes polite replies to your reviews.

• Website – build a fast, modern website for your business in minutes, with IBAX chat built in. Every enquiry lands in your app.

• One Inbox – see your WhatsApp conversations and leads in one place, reply yourself, or turn the bot back on with one tap.

SIMPLE TO START
1. Create your account with email or Google.
2. Tell IBAX about your business.
3. Say "I want WhatsApp automation" — IBAX sets it up with you, step by step.
4. Try the messaging services free for 3 days.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CONTROL
You choose what to connect and can disconnect any time. We never see your passwords. Delete your account and data any time in Settings.

Demo videos in English, Hindi and Marathi are inside the app.

Coming next: Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and email automation.

ibaxai — Intelligent Business Automation Expert.
```
